A LITTLE LESS UNKNOWN

You can start exactly
where you are.

Your first session is a chance to get to know each other.

01

Before you meet

Choose a quiet space if you’re meeting online. You can jot down what brought you here, or simply arrive with an open mind.

02

A conversation, at your pace

You can talk about what’s been on your mind and what you hope to get from your sessions. It’s okay to pause, ask questions, or say you’re unsure.

03

Find your fit

Ask about the psychologist’s approach, session fees, confidentiality, and cancellation policy. You can take time to decide whether you’d like another session.

A few things you might be wondering.

Do I need to know exactly what’s wrong?

No. You can begin with what you’re experiencing or what you’d like support with. You don’t need a diagnosis or a prepared speech.

Can I speak in a language I’m comfortable with?

Use the language filter to explore profiles. For a real service, confirm the practitioner’s supported languages before booking.

How do online sessions work?

A live service would provide a secure meeting link after booking. This version demonstrates scheduling and does not create video calls.

Is this a live healthcare service?

This is a working product demo with sample profiles, a user-provided listing, and example booking availability. It is not connected to real psychologists, payments, or a clinical service. Please don’t enter sensitive health information.
